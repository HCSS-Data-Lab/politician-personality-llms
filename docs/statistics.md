# Statistical conventions

Reverse-key the five negative Big Five items (`s2`, `s4`, `s6`, `s8`, `s10`) as 4 minus response, then average each trait's two items. Dark Triad pairs are positively keyed. Average repeats, and for speech conditions average speech-by-repeat observations within each politician. The open ensemble averages the four open models' politician-level scores before any correlation is computed; GPT is excluded from that ensemble.

Primary convergence is Pearson correlation; Spearman is retained as a check. A trait with a missing expert value drops only that politician/trait observation. Shared politician resamples preserve dependence across traits and across the two sides of a contrast. Aggregate convergence is the mean of trait correlations; its fraction of the expert ceiling is the mean of the trait-specific r/ceiling ratios. It is not the ratio of two overall means. Test-retest reliability is the one-way ICC(1,1), not a two-way panel-agreement ICC.

Directional bias follows the executed implementation: calculate each politician's model-minus-expert gap, divide by that politician's expert-panel trait SD, then average the standardized gaps. Missing or zero SDs do not enter that standardized average. This differs from dividing the overall average gap by the across-politician SD of the expert means. The code and reference outputs preserve the executed calculation.

Most bootstrap analyses use 2,000 percentile resamples, seed 20260706. The ideology appendix uses the September implementation: 5,000 resamples, seed 20260919, preserving sequential random-number consumption across its original cell order. The supplied adjusted NEGex values are loaded from the separately obtained public release; the original adjustment itself cannot be reconstructed from public aggregates. Its comparison with model outputs can be recomputed.

The language checks reproduce the grouping actually used for the current paper/reference tables: an authored country-to-dominant-language proxy, not verified transcript-level language identification. Some speeches use a different language or mix languages. Treat those checks as sensitivity to this proxy; they do not establish that model-family differences are independent of actual input language. Frozen speaker-recognition rates are also tabulated using that proxy where grouped by language.

Prominence statistics use the frozen Wikipedia and Wikidata measurements in `measurements/prominence_2026-07-06.csv`, restricted to the fixed public sample. The prominence, desirability, and optional metadata commands default to those historical counts. Fresh retrieval records its actual fetch date and can change tertile membership and statistics; select it explicitly with `--prominence`. See [prominence.md](prominence.md) for the source definitions and CC0 licensing.
